Contact & Reporting Guidance

CEOIS is a student prototype and is not an official electricity-department service.

How to Report a Problem in CEOIS

  1. Open the Report page.
  2. Enter your name and choose your area.
  3. Describe the electricity problem.
  4. Submit the form. The report will be saved for the project administrator to review.
Important: CEOIS does not automatically send a report to the electricity provider. For urgent electricity issues or to confirm an outage, contact your local electricity provider through its official contact channels.
